Privacy Policy for Laser

Last updated 19 September 2026

Laser is a personal todo app that time-blocks tasks around your calendar. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Laser server, no account to create, and no database — so there is nowhere for Laser to store your information even if it wanted to.

What Laser accesses

  • Google Calendar events, via the calendar.events scope. Laser reads the events on your primary calendar to find the gaps between them, and writes time blocks back into those gaps when you ask it to.
  • Todoist tasks, via a Todoist API token that you paste in yourself.

How that data is handled

Your browser talks to Google and Todoist directly. Nothing is routed through a server operated by Laser, because none exists. Calendar events, tasks, your Todoist token and your display preferences are cached in localStorage on the device you are using, and stay on that device.

Laser's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the time-blocking features described above. It is never sold, never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train any model.

What Laser does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No cookies set by Laser.
  • No data sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No email address, name, or payment information collected.

Third parties

Laser loads Google Identity Services from accounts.google.com to run the sign-in flow, and calls the Google Calendar and Todoist APIs. Those requests are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Todoist's Privacy Policy. The site is served as static files by Cloudflare Pages, which may log ordinary web request metadata such as IP addresses as part of serving and protecting the site.

Removing your data

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases everything Laser has stored, including the Todoist token. To revoke Laser's access to your Google account, visit Google Account permissions and remove it. Time blocks Laser already created on your calendar are ordinary calendar events and can be deleted in Google Calendar like any other.

Children

Laser is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
